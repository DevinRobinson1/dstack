# Plans and their arguments

Each plan here ships with a `.review-log.md` beside it. The log is the full adversarial review: every round's critique verbatim, what was accepted, what was rejected, and why.

The log is kept whole on purpose. A plan that says "reviewed and approved" tells you nothing. A plan that shows three rounds of a second model trying to break it, and shows which objections survived, is the only version of that claim worth anything.
